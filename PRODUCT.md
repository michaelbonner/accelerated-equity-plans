# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business leaders and equity compensation professionals at private and public companies who need experienced support administering, improving, or scaling equity programs. They visit the site to assess AEP's expertise, services, and credibility before starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Present Accelerated Equity Plans as a trusted, senior-level equity management partner. The site should help prospective clients understand AEP's capabilities, see evidence of the team's experience, and confidently take the next step toward an engagement.

## Brand Personality

Professional, authoritative, and business-focused. The experience should feel confident and approachable without becoming cute, playful, or casual.

## Anti-references

Avoid cute, whimsical, overly playful, or novelty-driven presentation. Avoid generic startup styling that weakens the firm's credibility or makes complex equity work feel simplistic.

## Design Principles

- Lead with expertise and clear business value.
- Make complex services easy to understand without diminishing their sophistication.
- Use concise, confident copy and purposeful calls to action.
- Demonstrate the depth of the team without overwhelming the primary page journey.
- Preserve a polished, credible experience across screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Maintain sufficient color contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, useful alternative text, and support for reduced motion where motion is present.
